Before you build
Please read this and scroll to the end.

POZi Foreman is a planning tool. It helps you organize a project, work out quantities, and put together a material list. It is not a substitute for professional judgment.

Foreman can be wrong

Foreman is AI and it makes mistakes. Before you buy anything or start work, verify:

  • measurements, quantities, and cut lengths
  • material suitability for your conditions
  • local building codes and permit requirements
  • manufacturer instructions and load ratings

Some work needs a professional

Have a qualified, licensed professional review anything structural, electrical, plumbing, gas, roofing, excavation, or load-bearing. Call 811 or your local utility-marking service before you dig. Nothing in POZi is engineering approval, a code ruling, or a permit. When in doubt, check with your local building department.
