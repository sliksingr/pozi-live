How it works

From an idea
to a loaded truck.

POZi turns a project you're describing out loud into an organized plan, a checked material list, and a path to buy it nearby.

1

Start with the project

Tell Foreman what you want to do. It asks one question at a time, and only when the answer actually changes the materials — the real stair height, the span, the finish you want.

Why it asksA guess at a measurement becomes a wrong cut list. Foreman would rather ask than assume.
2

Build the plan

When there's enough to work with, Foreman produces Build Notes: dimensions, cut list, fasteners and spacing, installation order, and what to check before you start.

The math is shown“84 linear ft ÷ 8 ft stock = 10.5, round up to 11, +1 waste = 12 boards.” You can check every number instead of trusting it.
3

Source the materials

The item list becomes a live product search. POZi returns real products from approved retailers, targeted to your ZIP so results are places you can actually drive to.

Local firstSet your location once and every search is geo-targeted. Retailers are an approved list — no marketplace resellers.
4

Keep it with the job

Notes, lists, and photos stay attached to the project. Come back next week, or next season, and the whole thing is still there.

POZi Disclaimer
Important information

POZi Foreman is a planning and sourcing tool. It helps organize projects and compare materials — it does not replace professional judgement.

Foreman can make mistakes. Always verify measurements, quantities, materials, local building codes, permits, safety requirements, and manufacturer instructions before purchasing or beginning a project.

Electrical, plumbing, excavation, roofing, structural, gas, load-bearing, and other safety-critical work should be reviewed by a qualified professional where appropriate. Call 811 or your local utility-marking service before digging.

POZi does not sell products directly. Pricing, availability, delivery, pickup, warranties, and returns are handled by third-party retailers and can change without notice.

POZi is actively developed and some features are in beta.
