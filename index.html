How it works

From an idea
to a loaded truck.

POZi turns a project you're describing out loud into an organized plan, a checked material list, and a path to buy it nearby.

1

Start with the project

Tell Foreman what you want to do. It asks one question at a time, and only when the answer actually changes the materials — the real stair height, the span, the finish you want.

Why it asksA guess at a measurement becomes a wrong cut list. Foreman would rather ask than assume.
2

Build the plan

When there's enough to work with, Foreman produces Build Notes: dimensions, cut list, fasteners and spacing, installation order, and what to check before you start.

The math is shown“84 linear ft ÷ 8 ft stock = 10.5, round up to 11, +1 waste = 12 boards.” You can check every number instead of trusting it.
3

Source the materials

The item list becomes a live product search. POZi returns real products from approved retailers, targeted to your ZIP so results are places you can actually drive to.

Local firstSet your location once and every search is geo-targeted. Retailers are an approved list — no marketplace resellers.
4

It stays a project

This is the part people miss: POZi isn't a search box. Every job is a project that holds its Foreman conversation, Build Notes, item list, and photos together. Come back next week or next season and the whole thing is still there — and contractors can share it straight to a client.

One place per jobNo more hunting through texts, photo rolls, and receipts to remember what you decided and what you bought.

Before you build
Please read this and scroll to the end.

POZi Foreman is a planning tool. It helps you organize a project, work out quantities, and put together a material list. It is not a substitute for professional judgment.

Foreman can be wrong

Foreman is AI and it makes mistakes. Before you buy anything or start work, verify:

  • measurements, quantities, and cut lengths
  • material suitability for your conditions
  • local building codes and permit requirements
  • manufacturer instructions and load ratings

Some work needs a professional

Have a qualified, licensed professional review anything structural, electrical, plumbing, gas, roofing, excavation, or load-bearing. Call 811 or your local utility-marking service before you dig. Nothing in POZi is engineering approval, a code ruling, or a permit. When in doubt, check with your local building department.
